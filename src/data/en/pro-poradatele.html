David Navrátil is Chief Economist of Česká spořitelna (Erste Group), where he has led the Economic and Strategic Research team since 2008. Born in the Ostrava region, he started his career at the Czech National Bank. He explains complicated topics the Ostrava way: straight to the point and without fog. He co-authors the Prosperity and Financial Health Index and the Stronger Regions project, which show where Czechia and its regions lag behind and where they lead. His focus is prosperity in an age of polycrisis, when ageing, artificial intelligence, expensive energy and war hit at once. To the forecast he adds scenarios of different futures and a way through them: resilience and the ability to reinvent, that is, to change in time what has stopped working. He speaks plainly, with numbers people still remember on their way home, and with humour that never costs precision. The Czech tech and business outlet CzechCrunch named him one of the two most visible macroeconomists in the Czech media.

English, long

for proceedings and for the host · 216 words

David Navrátil is Chief Economist of Česká spořitelna (Erste Group). Born in the Ostrava region and a graduate of VŠB-Technical University of Ostrava, he started as an analyst at the Czech National Bank, joined Česká spořitelna in 2004 and has been its Chief Economist since 2008, leading the Economic and Strategic Research team. Ostrava taught him to say things straight, so even difficult topics come without fog and without scaremongering. He co-authors the Prosperity and Financial Health Index, which benchmarks Czechia against Europe, and the Stronger Regions project, which asks why some regions do better than their starting position would suggest. His question is how to support prosperity in an age of polycrisis. That is why he builds the Megatrends and Uncertainties catalogue, setting out in advance for every uncertainty the signals that will show which way things are moving. At conferences and in strategy sessions with leadership teams he adds to the forecast scenarios of different futures and a way through them: resilience and the ability to reinvent. He gives over 50 talks a year and has commented on the economy in the media for more than 15 years; CzechCrunch named him one of the two most visible macroeconomists in the Czech media. He writes the newsletter Peníze, procenta a prosperita (Money, Percentages and Prosperity).

David Navrátil je hlavní ekonom České spořitelny, kde od roku 2008 vede tým ekonomických a strategických analýz. Rodák z Ostravska začínal v České národní bance. K prognóze přidává scénáře různých budoucností a cestu přes odolnost a schopnost reinvence: umět včas změnit to, co přestalo fungovat. Mluví lidsky, s čísly, která si lidé pamatují ještě cestou domů, a s humorem, který neubírá na přesnosti. CzechCrunch ho označil za jednoho ze dvou mediálně nejvýraznějších makroekonomů v Česku.

Introduction for the host

Half a minute before the first word

An economist is someone who can explain to you today, with great sophistication, what the world, the economy and interest rates will look like a year from now. A year later, he will explain with even greater sophistication, and perfect precision, exactly why he got it wrong. Our next guest makes forecasts too. He just adds an insurance policy: several scenarios, the numbers that will tell us which way things are heading, and resilience whichever way they go. Chief Economist of Česká spořitelna and co-author of the Czech Prosperity Index. Please welcome David Navrátil.
